The challenge

Pricing gets harder with every new clinic.

Every location brings another fee schedule, another set of decisions, and another chance for changes to get lost between headquarters and the clinic.

1
Scattered fee schedules

Pricing lives across different systems and local workflows.

2
Slow rollouts

Coordinating updates location by location takes too much time.

3
Limited visibility

It is hard to know which clinics have reviewed or completed changes.

The idea

One decision. A clear path to every clinic.

Your group sets the direction. Each location keeps its context. Vet INC brings the plan, review, and rollout into one visible process.

How it works

A straightforward path from decision to rollout.

Pricing work should be easy to follow across the entire group, from the central team to each clinic.

01

Bring your clinics together

Set up a group view of locations and fee schedules so your team starts with the full picture.

02

Review and plan changes

Compare pricing, decide what should change, and keep clinic-specific context in view.

03

Coordinate the rollout

Work through approvals and updates with a clear record of progress for each location.

04

See where things stand

Return to one place to check what is complete and what still needs attention.

Your clinics are different. Your pricing process should account for those differences without losing the group-wide view.
